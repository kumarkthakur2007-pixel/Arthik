🌐
The Vision
Arthika is a premium, privacy-centric financial management tool built on a Zero-Footprint architecture. The app operates 100% offline, using IndexedDB to ensure your financial data never leaves your device. In a world where personal data is exploited, Arthika stands apart.
⚡
Core Capabilities
Insight Engine — Analyzes spending patterns to provide intelligent feedback on financial health.
Gamified Discipline — Dynamic Financial Score that rewards consistent tracking and high savings rates.
Visual Analytics — Heatmaps, Category Trends and 6-month comparisons to visualize spending habits.
Smart Automation — Quick Add widget, streak tracking, and goal prediction engine.
